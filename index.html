    MAGNET.

    Collect scrap, charge Magnetic Theft, and outgrow your rivals.

    Move with the mouse or WASD. On touchscreens, tap a destination or drag to steer.

    Scrap adds mass. Gold adds ten. Red energy charges Magnetic Theft. With a full charge, hold Space or Attack to aim and release to fire.

    Large magnets lose more of their diameter when hit. Anyone can contest the salvage after it lands. The damaged magnet has a brief theft guard and can reclaim its own salvage after five seconds.

    Keep clear of Flux Spikes as you grow. Heat weakens your field, water and acid corrode your shell, and cryogenic vents make you brittle.

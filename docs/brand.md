# MAGNET visual identity

MAGNET is a top-down cartoon scrapyard. Thick black ink, exaggerated faces, enamel paint, and comic impact poses give the equipment the energy of a late-2000s browser arcade game. The steel horseshoe with a red pole and a blue pole is the shared silhouette in the arena, app mark, and menu illustrations.

## Drawing and color

Draw exterior character contours at about 4–6 pixels on the 128-pixel reference. Use interior strokes around 1.5–2.5 pixels. Separate steel, enamel, and rubber with hard shadow planes and thin reflected highlights. Keep the jaw open, bevel the pole tips, and use asymmetry in the eyes, brows, mouths, and decals. Reinforce the back and shoulders as a magnet grows so its shape conveys its weight.

Use saturated cel color, hard shadow planes, and crisp white or pale-yellow highlights. The shared palette starts with magnetic red `#FF3030`, electric blue `#238CFF`, toxic green `#8FFF20`, electric purple `#A740FF`, industrial yellow `#FFD323`, hot orange `#FF7820`, steel `#9CA8B5`, pure black `#000000`, and white `#FFFFFF`. Luminous pickups and magnetic energy use soft gradients. Put the strongest color and darkest ink on objects the player can act on. Give the ground broader, quieter plates, seams, and district markings. [Color tokens](../brand/tokens.json)

## Characters and play

Keep each character’s signature chassis, decal, and expression. Seat the eyes, brows, and mouth in the chassis faceplate. Its stage-specific socket carries every facial layer through rotation, stretch, recoil, and growth. Pupils share a direction in world space and stay inside their individual eye apertures. They favor visible opponents, then useful resources, then travel. Relaxed and curious characters may blink. Growth adds steel, rivets, and layered plates across six stages.

Use one of sixteen expressions to coordinate the eyes, brows and mouth. A rival can prompt concern before a hit. An imminent threat widens the eyes and tightens the pupils. Heat, frost, corrosion, injury, and a weapon’s charge can carry their own visible marks while the character reacts to something else. [Living Faces](rc7a-living-faces.md)

Ultimate Form retains the physical Colossus silhouette and adds gold reinforcement, steel highlights, glowing seams, and sparks on collection. Keep each original chassis, signature colors, and decal. An unthreatened Ultimate magnet wears an overconfident grin. Instability turns the grin nervous, then tightens it into clenched teeth. Open the eyes and mouth wide for a claimant in panic. Give the blast a white electrical core, black ink, orange and yellow energy, and a separate crimson safety boundary. [Ultimate roster](evidence/rc7b/ultimate-roster.webp) · [Overload expressions](evidence/rc7b/overload-expressions.webp)

Use a short compression at takeoff, restrained stretch in motion, and a small settle on stopping. A new plate snaps into place at a growth threshold. The arena model supplies movement, damage, and collection geometry. A heavy hit adds a longer crack, recoil, and detached steel plates, while claimable combat salvage keeps its own gold-and-steel shapes. Charge, guard, and damage stay recognizable when they overlap.

Energy keeps a pale lightning bolt and a segmented red orbit at distance. Gold uses an angular nugget silhouette. Scrap simplifies to glossy, multicolored cores. Furnaces, enclosed water pools, chemical vats, cryogenic machinery, and jagged Flux fins distinguish the hazards by shape and remain inside their danger outlines. Give the six districts their own mechanical floor stamps and props. Recess the surface seams and use the brightest colors for interactive objects. ## Interface and sound

Use the bundled Open Sans display and regular faces. Titles use outlined, slanted lettering. Buttons use cast rims, hard highlights, and a short press. Give the charge meter segment marks and the Attack button a legible label in each phase. The title artwork uses the game cast. Arena victory, defeat, and Endless have separate illustrations.

Keep the two-note pole cue and synth workshop palette. Use quick metallic pickup notes, electrical charge and guard sounds, and a deeper transient for severe impacts. Give the countdown three distinct beats and keep the impact and survival or victory cues legible in the mix. Reduced motion keeps expressions, ability states, telegraphs, hazard outlines, and the authoritative wave rim visible.
